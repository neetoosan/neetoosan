DETAILED PROFILE

From Curiosity to Code • One Bug at a Time

I’m Israel Oyekanmi, also known as Neetoosan — a naturally curious creative who stumbled into code and never looked back. My journey started with a simple question: "What's harder to understand than a lady?" Someone said Python. I laughed, then I tried it. Turns out, they weren't entirely wrong but I was completely hooked. 🐍

What began as curiosity quickly became an obsession. I dove into Python, then wandered into game development with C++, discovering the visceral thrill of real-time state synchronization, math, and interactive graphics. That led me to PySide6, where I started crafting desktop tools that actually solved daily operational headaches. But I wanted my work to reach everywhere — which led me to Flutter and Dart. Cross-platform apps, one elegant architecture, endless possibilities.

"The road wasn't smooth. I pitched to schools, students, and organizations and heard 'no' more times than I can count. The feedback was brutal, the rejections stung. But every 'no' fueled me instead of breaking me."

Eventually, I started selling my applications. Not because it was easy, but because I refused to quit. Today, I build software that performs in the real world: backend systems handling thousands of database records, competitive matchmaking platforms with sub-100ms player pairings, binocular vision therapy mobile apps, and anonymous AI reporting systems. I bring that same fierce curiosity to every project — always learning, always refining, always asking "what if?"
